Udzorong Central School is a co-educational school located in Udzorong Gewog under Trashigang Dzongkhag, Bhutan. The school was established in 1994 and provides education to students from different parts of the community. As a central school, it provides boarding facilities and focuses on both academic learning and the overall development of students.
